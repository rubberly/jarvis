# JARVIS

A JARVIS-style holographic assistant that runs in the browser.

**Live site:** https://rubberly.github.io/jarvis/

- Animated arc-reactor HUD with a British speaking voice
- Voice commands with the wake word "Jarvis" (Chrome or Edge)
- Hand tracking through your webcam (MediaPipe): pinch to grab the hologram, turn your hand to rotate it, pinch with both hands to resize
- Timers, device diagnostics, maths, and HUD colour schemes

Click **Initialize**, then **Hands** to turn on the camera. Everything runs locally in your browser; no video leaves your device.
